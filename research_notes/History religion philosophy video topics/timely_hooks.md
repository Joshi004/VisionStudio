## 2. Religious and cultural dates (Nov 2026 – Mar 2027)

### Takeaway
The window is packed with festival dates:
- **Gita Jayanti:** 20 Dec 2026.
- **Christmas:** 25 Dec.
- **Makar Sankranti and the start of Magh Mela:** 15 Jan 2027.
- **Lunar New Year and Mauni Amavasya on the same day:** 6 Feb.
- **Ramadan starts:** about 8 Feb.
- **Vasant Panchami:** 11 Feb.
- **Mahashivratri and the end of Magh Mela:** 6 Mar.
- **Holi:** 22 or 23 Mar.

The Nashik-Trimbakeshwar Simhastha Kumbh formally *began* on 31 Oct 2026, but its big bathing days are in Aug–Sep 2027, outside the window. Ram Navami (15 Apr 2027) and Easter (28 Mar 2027) also fall just outside it.

## 3. Recent archaeology and history news (2026) that viewers are curious about

### Takeaway
In India, the most timely stories are:
- **Dwarka:** the ASI may restart underwater exploration at Dwarka / Bet Dwarka from **December 2026**. It would be the first proper underwater search since 2007, at the legendary city of Krishna.
- **Rakhigarhi:** ancient-DNA work on newly excavated burials is under way.
- **Keezhadi:** a live Centre vs. Tamil Nadu dispute over the findings.

Globally, the hooks are Pompeii's sealed cupboard (29 Oct 2026), new Saqqara tombs, and the Grand Egyptian Museum's first anniversary (1 Nov 2026). No credible new Indus-script decipherment was found.

### Cited Findings
**India**
- **Dwarka / Bet Dwarka underwater exploration "could begin by December" 2026, subject to ASI approval.** It would be the first proper underwater search since 2007.
  - The team is expected to work with Prof. Alok Tripathi, former ASI ADG, who led the 2025 onshore and offshore work.
  - A source says the "final analysis of the site will be completed this time."
  - The 2007 dig yielded terracotta objects, beads, bangles, copper rings, iron ingots and pottery.
  - The ASI has done underwater fieldwork on only 11 occasions since 1981.
  - Source: [New Indian Express, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Nov/13/asi-set-to-approve-deep-dive-for-exploration-at-dwarka-bet-dwarka)
- **Rakhigarhi:** In the 2025–26 season, the ASI uncovered 8 burials at Mound No. 7 (a cemetery). Three complete skeletons were handed to the Anthropological Survey of India for ancient-DNA, isotope and paleopathology studies, with BSIP Lucknow, UCL and BHU. Prof. Gyaneshwer Chaubey called it a major step in reconstructing the genomic history of the Harappans — [PIB, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2276489&lang=1&reg=3); [DD News](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/rakhigarhi-human-remains-sent-for-advanced-scientific-study-to-uncover-secrets-of-indus-saraswati-civilization/). (Government sources use the term "Indus-Saraswati Civilization".)
- **Keezhadi:** On 24 Sep 2026, Tamil Nadu Archaeology Minister A. Rajmohan defended the findings as scientific. He challenged the Centre to form an expert panel after Union Culture Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat questioned whether history can be fixed from a single finding, and urged the Centre to publish the research. He cited Tamil-Brahmi inscriptions on potsherds that name ordinary people — [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2026/Sep/24/keezhadi-findings-backed-by-science-form-panel-if-in-doubt-says-minister-a-rajmohan-to-centre). A Sep 2026 journal paper argues for urban literacy at Keezhadi before 600 BCE — [Int. Journal of History](https://doi.org/10.22271/27069109.2026.v8.i9a.872) (a minor journal; treat with caution).

**Global**
- **Pompeii (announced 29 Oct 2026):** An unopened wooden cupboard was found in House IX 11, 7 on Via dell'Abbondanza. It held 38 bronze coins (36 from Nero's reign, 64–67 AD), a bag of 24 childhood amulets called *crepundia* (depicting Bes and Mercury, hands, feet and pine cones), tweezers, a lamp and an incense burner. The remains of two young wood mice were found nearby. The Pompeii Archaeological Park published a 37-page report — [Earth.com](https://www.earth.com/science/pompeii-cupboard-charms-coins-mice/); [Ancient Dispatch roundup](https://ancientdispatch.com/weekly-roundup-24-31-october-2026/)
- **Saqqara:**
  - The 4,000-year-old tomb of the official Sekhentiu-Ptah, with a false door, burial shafts and more than 100 ushabtis — [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/archaeologists-discover-a-4000-year-old-tomb-with-a-false-door-at-egypts-ancient-saqqara-necropolis-180989438/)
