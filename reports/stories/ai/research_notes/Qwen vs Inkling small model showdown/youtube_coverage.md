# YouTube coverage of Inkling 2 Mini and Qwen3.8-27B, and sound-bite candidates (as of 27 Nov 2026)

**How this was gathered.** I scraped YouTube search results and watch pages directly on 27 Nov 2026, so views, upload dates and caption types below come from the YouTube page metadata. I pulled time-coded transcripts for about 45 videos with `youtube-transcript-api`, then YouTube started blocking further transcript requests from my IP. I did **not** watch any video. That means for every quote below: (a) whether the speaker's face is on camera is **not verified**; (b) wording comes from captions. Where the captions are auto-generated, names are often garbled (for example "Quen", "Cwen", "Cuen", "Kuen" all mean Qwen; "Inclined Too Many" means Inkling 2 Mini), so every auto-caption quote should be checked against the audio before use. Timestamps are caption-segment start times (minutes:seconds) and can be off by about ±2 seconds. Upload dates are YouTube's Pacific-time `uploadDate`. View counts are what the page showed on 27 Nov 2026 and will keep changing.

Video URL format for every ID below: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<ID>`.

## 1. Which YouTube videos review or test Inkling 2 Mini in launch week (20–27 Nov 2026)?

### Takeaway
Launch week produced dozens of videos. The most-viewed are hype-framed one-shot app/game showcases, led by Bijan Bowen ("INSANE", about 168K views) and WorldofAI ("BEATS Qwen 3.8 27B", about 97K). Most of the useful data comes from smaller channels that ran repeated, timed tests (Luke's Dev Lab, The Dark Factory, Samuel Gregory, DIY Smart Code, AI Coding Daily, Zero to MVP). Their usual verdict: roughly tied with Qwen3.8-27B on quality, faster in the cloud and often on NVIDIA, but with loops, crashes and slowness on local setups at launch.

## 3. Did anyone reproduce the flight-simulator demo or test on a 24GB/32GB Mac?

### Takeaway
One reviewer (JeredBlu, 36GB Mac) reproduced the flight-sim idea offline in 11 minutes, but only in one harness. In two other harnesses the model looped for 23 minutes to over an hour without writing code, and macOS used about 20 GB of swap. I found no test on a real 24GB or 32GB Mac. One reviewer simulated a 32GB Mac and saw Ollama crash repeatedly, and concluded Qwen is "the safer bet" at that size.

### Cited Findings
- **Official demo.** It ran offline on a "MacBook Pro (M5 Pro, 48GB)" in airplane mode and "built a playable flight simulator in a few minutes". The video plays mostly at 15× speed with a real-time timer. — [Thinking Machines launch post](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/). The prompt and coding tool were not published, according to [VibeGamer](https://vibegamer.io/blog/inkling-2-mini-games). JeredBlu instead says the team appears to use "open code running Inkling Too Many via Ollama" and that his prompt "was the same prompt that the Thinking Machines team did on the airplane" (~1:01, ~3:36). These conflict: the prompt's public availability is unverified.

## 4. Hype titles vs independent data; what tests reviewers typically run

### Takeaway
